# DEEPWAKE feature audit

## Expedition inventory

The starting workspace contained the design brief. The finished expedition uses a 120 Hz simulation, an authored course from the workshop spawn at x = 4 to the lift at x = 208, three relay circuits, three archives, three salvage caches, four machines across three enemy types, and a staged lift ending. Rendering uses the local Three.js 0.160.1 distribution.

## Final verification

- **19 deterministic tests passed.** They exercise the production simulation and record store. Both complete-route tests reject ordinary damage, shield absorption and recovery. The controller supplies movement and action inputs and reads state for timing.
- **Two rendered Chromium completions passed.** The route without discoveries took **72.44 seconds** of active expedition time. The full-discovery route took **78.41 seconds**, retained all three archives and all three caches, and activated A, B and C. Both finished with zero recoveries. Browser replay advances fixed steps in batches and renders sample frames along each route.
- Keyboard play, CDP multitouch, input cancellation, focus loss, sound preference, reduced motion, retry, restart, archive dismissal, the scrollable result panel, record reload and committed-world rollback were exercised in the browser.
- Visual inspection covered the workshop, reservoir crossing, foundry cover and counterweight, ascent platform, daylight terrace, and completion overlay. Layout checks used **1440 × 900**, **390 × 844**, and **740 × 360**.
- The optimized input traces measure reachability. The 8–12 minute first-player pacing target still needs a human playtest. Sound verification measured generated output and lifecycle behavior. Perceived mix balance, physical mobile hardware and sustained GPU frame rate were outside the measured checks.
